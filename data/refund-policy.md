# E-Commerce Refund Policy

1. Standard products may be refunded within 30 calendar days of delivery.
2. The product must be unused and in resalable condition.
3. Final-sale and clearance products are not refundable.
4. An order may receive only one successful refund.
5. Damaged products may qualify for a refund within 14 calendar days of delivery.
6. Refund amount cannot exceed the original order amount.
7. Orders flagged for fraud are not eligible for automatic refunds.
8. The order must belong to the authenticated/identified customer.
9. Orders that have not been delivered are not eligible for a standard refund.
10. The agent must verify customer and order information before processing a refund.
11. The agent must never approve a refund based solely on the customer's statement.
12. If the request contains conflicting information or cannot be safely verified, the request must go to HUMAN_REVIEW.
13. Every refund decision must have a recorded reason.
14. The system must never call process_refund before eligibility validation succeeds.
